# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want tonEMU to be a friendly, respectful and welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, background, experience level, gender, identity, ability, nationality or beliefs. We pledge to keep our community free from harassment.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept feedback politely. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Stay on topic and keep issues and discussions useful.
- Respect the project's [Anti-Piracy Policy](ANTI-PIRACY.md).

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, bullying or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory, sexual or violent language or imagery.
- Trolling, spam or repeatedly derailing discussions.
- Sharing other people's private information without their permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a community like this.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, comments, wiki), release pages and any other official tonEMU channel. It also applies when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit or lock comments, issues and pull requests that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they find harmful. Maintainers will try to be fair and to explain their decision.

## Reporting

Report problems to **jedplatforms@gmail.com**. Please include links or screenshots if you can. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity will be kept confidential as far as possible.
